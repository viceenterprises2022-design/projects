### 6. Risk Factors & The "Comfort Trap"

**IGI (Gemological Institute) Analysis: A Paradox of Quality**
IGI presents a complex risk case. It is an **asset-light cash machine** with 70% operating margins, yet its P/E multiple has halved to 26x due to structural shifts:
*   **Price Collapse:** Lab-grown diamond prices have crashed 80%.
*   **Oversupply:** Indian CVD reactor capacity is projected to hit 13,000–14,000 by 2026.
*   **GIA Shift:** The Gemological Institute of America has **stopped grading lab diamonds on the 4C scale**, classifying them simply as "laboratory-grown," a move that threatens IGI’s certification relevance.

**The Psychological Risk**
Investors are warned against the "Comfort Trap." Waiting for absolute geopolitical or economic certainty is a "method of ensuring you never buy anything cheaply." The best prices are paid when headlines are most alarming.
