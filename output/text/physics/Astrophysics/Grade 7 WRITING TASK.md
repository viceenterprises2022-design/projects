ANSWERS
Ans 1: The Big Bang Theory explains how the universe began around 13.8 billion years
ago, when the universe started from an extremely hot, dense point. It then expanded
rapidly—not like an explosion in space, but an expansion of space itself. As it
expanded, it cooled down, allowing particles to form atoms, then stars, galaxies, and
planets.
Ans  2: The universe is expanding.
Ans  3: Most asteroids are found in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter.
Ans   4:  They are mostly made of rock and metal.
Ans   5:  When a comet gets close to the Sun, it heats up, and the ice inside it turns into gas
and dust, forming a glowing head and a tail.
Ans   6: Halley’s Comet.
Ans   7: Gas and dust.
Ans   8: A meteoroid is a small piece of rock or metal traveling through space, smaller
than an asteroid.
Ans   9: A shooting star is not actually a star. It is a Meteor—a bright streak of light you see when a
small space rock burns up in the sky.
Ans   10: It heats up and glows due to friction.
