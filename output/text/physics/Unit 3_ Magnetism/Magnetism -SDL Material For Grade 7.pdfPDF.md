Theory of Magnetism
The Magnetism explains how certain materials can attract or repel each other without
touching.
  Basic Idea
Magnetism is caused by moving electric charges inside atoms. Every material is made of
tiny particles called atoms, and inside them, electrons move. This movement creates tiny
magnetic effects.


In most materials → these tiny magnetic effects cancel out
In magnetic materials (like iron) → they line up in the same direction, creating a strong
magnet
  Magnetic Domains
Inside magnetic materials, there are small regions called domains:
  Each domain acts like a tiny magnet


In an unmagnetized object → domains are arranged randomly
In a magnet → domains are aligned in one direction
👉 When domains align, the material becomes a magnet.
  Magnetic Poles
Every magnet has two ends called poles:
  North pole (N)
South pole (S)

Important rules:
Like poles repel (N–N, S–S)

  Unlike poles attract (N–S)
You cannot get a magnet with only one pole—if you cut a magnet, each piece will still have
both poles.
  Magnetic Field
A magnet creates an invisible region around it called a magnetic field.
  This is where magnetic force can be felt
  The force is strongest near the poles
It acts without physical contact

  Earth as a Magnet
The Earth’s magnetic field makes our planet behave like a giant magnet.
  This is why a compass always points North-South

It helps in navigation
  Temporary vs Permanent Magnetism
  Temporary magnets: Lose magnetism quickly (e.g., soft iron)
  Permanent magnets: Keep magnetism for a long time (e.g., steel)
In Simple Words
Magnetism happens when tiny magnetic parts inside a material work together in the same
direction, creating a force that can pull or push objects without touching them.
