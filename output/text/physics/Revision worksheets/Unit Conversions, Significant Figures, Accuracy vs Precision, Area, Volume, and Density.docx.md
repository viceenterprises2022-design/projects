Grade 7 Physics – Practice Questions
Answer the following questions. Total time: 30 minutes.
Topics: Unit Conversions, Significant Figures, Accuracy vs Precision, Area, Volume, and Density.
1. Unit Conversion – Easy
Convert the following measurements into the indicated units:
a) 3.5 kg = _______ g
b) 2500 mL = _______ L
c) 120 cm = _______ m
2. Significant Figures – Easy
How many significant figures are in each of the following?
a) 0.00340
b) 150.0
c) 7000
3. Sig Fig Calculation – Medium
Perform the following operations using correct significant figures:
a) 12.4 + 3.56
b) 15.23 × 2.1
c) 105.4 ÷ 0.025
4. Volume, Density, Precision – Medium
A student measures the mass of a cube as 85.2 g and each side of the cube is 3.00 cm.
a) Calculate the volume of the cube.
b) Find the density.
c) The actual density of the material is 9.00 g/cm³. Comment on the accuracy and precision of the student’s result.
5. Integrated Application
Two students measured the volume of the same irregular stone using a displacement method:
Student A got: 40 mL, 41 mL, 39 mL
Student B got: 40 mL, 35 mL, 45 mL
The mass of the stone is 160 g.
a) Who is more precise? Who is more accurate, assuming the correct volume is 40 mL?
b) Calculate the average density for each student’s data.
c) Which student’s data should be trusted more and why?
