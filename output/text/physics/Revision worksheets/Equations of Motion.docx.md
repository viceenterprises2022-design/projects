Criteria A: Knowing & Understanding
A toy car moves 10 m north and then 6 m south.
What is the distance covered?
What is the displacement?
A toy car travels 15 m in 5 s on a flat floor.
Calculate its speed.
If it is moving in a straight line, what is its velocity?
Two students took different routes to reach the same school gate from the same starting point. Who would have a greater displacement, and why?
Two cars are moving at the same speed, but one is going north and the other is going east. Do they have the same velocity? Why or why not?
Criteria B: Inquiring & Designing
A toy car is released on a ramp and travels 2 m in 4 s. The same car is then pushed by hand and travels 2 m in 2 s.
Compare the two motions.
Which one shows greater acceleration? Why?
Two toy cars are tested:
Car A moves at a constant speed of 0.5 m/s.
Car B starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 0.25 m/s² for 4 s.
Which car covers more distance in 4 s?
If a cyclist goes in a circular path and returns to the starting point, what will be their average velocity? Explain with reasoning.
Criteria C: Processing & Evaluating
(Scenarios using equations of motion)
A toy car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 0.5 m/s² for 6 s.
Use the equation v = u + at to find its final velocity.
A toy car starts from rest and accelerates at 1 m/s² for 5 s.
Use the equation s = ut + ½at² to find the distance travelled.
A toy car moves with a velocity of 2 m/s and accelerates at 0.5 m/s² for 8 s.
Find the final velocity.
A toy car moves with an initial velocity of 3 m/s, accelerates at 1 m/s² for 4 s.
Find the distance travelled.
A toy car accelerates from rest at 2 m/s².
How long will it take to reach 10 m/s?
