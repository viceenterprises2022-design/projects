Pressure in Fluids Practice questions:
Numericals on Pressure in Fluids
1. A force of 50 N acts on an area of 2 m². Find the pressure.
2. A force of 100 N acts on 0.5 m². Find pressure.
3. Pressure in a liquid is 500 Pa on an area of 4 m². Find the force.
4. A pressure of 300 Pa is produced by a force of 150 N. Find the area.
5. Comparing Pressure
A boy weighing 400 N stands on:
Both feet: total area = 0.04 m²
One foot: area = 0.02 m²
Numericals on Hydraulic Press (Pascal’s Law)
1. Small piston area = 2 cm², large piston area = 20 cm². Force on small piston = 50 N. What is the force on the large piston?
2. A1 = 5 cm², A2 = 50 cm², F2 = 1000 N. Find the force on small piston.
3. F1 = 200 N, A1 = 4 cm², F2 = 2000 N. What is the Area of Large Piston?
4. Car Lift
A mechanic applies 300 N on a piston of area 3 cm² in a car lift. The large piston area = 60 cm². What is the force on the large piston which lifts the car up?
5. Ratio Question
If the large piston area is 15 times the small piston area and the applied force is 80 N, find output force.
Key idea: Same pressure → bigger area gives bigger force
