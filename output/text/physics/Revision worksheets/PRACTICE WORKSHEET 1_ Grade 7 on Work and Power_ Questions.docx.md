PRACTICE WORKSHEET: 1
Grade 7
Topic: Work and Power
A student pushes a box with a force of 20 N over a distance of 5 m. Calculate the work done.
A girl lifts a school bag weighing 40 N to a height of 1.5 m. Find the work done.
A force of 15 N moves a trolley 8 m along the floor. Calculate the work done.
A worker does 250 J of work while pushing a cart by 10 m. Calculate the force applied.
A crane lifts a load with a force of 500 N and does 4000 J of work. Find the height the load was lifted.
A boy pushes a wall with a force of 100 N for 2 minutes, but the wall does not move. Calculate the work done.
A student does 200 J of work in 10 seconds. Calculate the power produced.
A machine uses 1500 J of energy in 30 seconds. Find its power.
A crane does 6000 J of work in 2 minutes. Calculate the power generated.
Light bulb has a power rating of 60 W. How much work does it do in 5 seconds?
Motor produces 80 W of power for 25 seconds. Calculate the work done by the motor.
A boy climbs stairs doing 900 J of work in 15 seconds, while his friend does the same work in 10 seconds. Who is more powerful?
