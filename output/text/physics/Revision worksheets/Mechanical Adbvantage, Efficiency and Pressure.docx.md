Grade 7 Physics Practice Questions:
A lever has an effort of 20 N and produces a load of 60 N. What is its mechanical advantage?
A pulley system has an MA of 4. If the effort applied is 15 N, what load can it lift?
A machine has an MA of 5, but its velocity ratio (VR) is 6.Calculate its efficiency.
A machine has a VR of 4 and is 75% efficient. Find its mechanical advantage.
A hydraulic press has a small piston area of 4 cm² and a large piston area of 20 cm².
If 10 N of force is applied on the small piston, what force is produced on the large piston?
A brick rests on the ground with dimensions 20 cm × 10 cm × 5 cm. On which face will it exert the greatest pressure? Explain.
A boy standing on one foot exerts more pressure than when standing on two. Why?
A force of 50 N acts on an area of 2 m². Calculate the pressure.
A machine requires 40 J of input work but gives only 28 J of useful output work. What is its efficiency?
A student says, “If a machine has high MA, it must also be highly efficient.” Is this always true? Explain with reasoning.
