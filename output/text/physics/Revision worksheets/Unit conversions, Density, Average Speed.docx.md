Grade 7 Boarder Support – Physics:
Differentiate between fundamental and derived quantities with two examples each.
Convert 3.5 km into meters and 2500 g into kilograms.
A student measures the length of a rod three times and records: 20.0 cm, 20.1 cm, and 19.9 cm.
Comment on the precision and accuracy of these measurements.
You want to test how the length of a pendulum affects its time period.
Identify the independent variable, dependent variable, and controlled variables in this investigation.
Calculate the area of a rectangle with length 8 cm and breadth 5 cm, and the volume of a cuboid with dimensions 4 cm × 3 cm × 2 cm.
A solid has a mass of 250 g and a volume of 50 cm³. Find its density.
Define least count and explain its importance in measurement.
Give one example each of a systematic error and a random error while measuring length, mass, or time.
Explain what parallax error is with a real-life example.
A car travels 60 km in the first hour and 40 km in the second hour.
Calculate its average speed.
If the car was moving at instantaneous speed of 80 km/h at some point, explain the difference between instantaneous speed and average speed.
