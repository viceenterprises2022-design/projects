PRACTICE WORKSHEET: 3
Grade 7
Topic: Three Equations of Motion
A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 3 m/s2 for 8 seconds. Find its final velocity.
A cyclist moving at 5 m/s increases her speed at a rate of 2 m/s2  for 6 seconds. Calculate the final velocity.
A train moving at 25 m/s slows down uniformly with an acceleration of −1.5 m/s2 for 10 seconds. Find its final velocity.
A ball is thrown vertically upward with an initial velocity of 20 m/s. Taking acceleration due to gravity as −9.8 m/s2, find its velocity after 1.5 seconds.
A motorcycle moving at 12 m/s accelerates uniformly and reaches a final velocity of 30 m/s in 6 seconds. Find the acceleration.
A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 4 m/s24\,\text{m/s}^24m/s2 for 5 seconds. Calculate the distance travelled.
A cyclist moving at 6 m/s accelerates uniformly at 2 m/s2 for 8 seconds. Find the distance travelled.
A train moving at 20 m/s slows down uniformly with an acceleration of −2 m/s2 for 6 seconds. Calculate the distance travelled during this time.
A ball is thrown vertically upward with an initial velocity of 15 m/s. Find the height reached after 2 seconds. Take g=−9.8 m/s2.
A bus moving at 10 m/s travels for 12 seconds with a uniform acceleration of 1.5 m/s2. Find the distance covered.
A car accelerates uniformly from 10 m/s to 30 m/s over a distance of 100 m. Find the acceleration.
A train moving at 25 m/s comes to rest uniformly in a distance of 250 m. Find the retardation.
A bike starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 3 m/s2. Find the distance travelled when its velocity becomes 18 m/s.
A stone is dropped from a height and reaches the ground with a velocity of 28 m/s. Taking g=9.8 m/s2, find the height from which it was dropped.
An airplane moving at 80 m/s slows down uniformly at −5 m/s2 before stopping. Calculate the distance covered before it stops.
