PRACTICE WORKSHEET: 4
Grade 7
Topic: Pressure  and Pascal’s Law
1. A girl of mass 48 kg stands on one high-heel shoe. The area of the heel touching the ground is 2 cm2.Find the pressure exerted on the ground.
(Take g=10 m/s2)
2. Box A has a base area of 0.4 m2 and weight 120 N.
Box B has a base area of 0.1 m2 and weight 80 N.
Find the pressure exerted by each box.
Which box exerts greater pressure?
3. A machine exerts a force of 15,000 N on a surface and produces a pressure of 300,000 Pa. Find the contact area.
4.  A small piston of area 0.02 m2 is used in a hydraulic lift. A force of 200 N is applied on it.
a). Find the pressure produced.    b).  If the same pressure acts on a large piston of area 0.5 m2, find the force produced on the large piston.
5. A camel of mass 600 kg stands on the sand using all four feet. Each foot has an area of 250 cm2. Find the pressure exerted on the sand.
(Take g=10 m/s2).
6. A metal block weighs 900 N. It can rest on two different faces:
Face A area =0.3 m2
Face B area =0.05 m2
Find:  a). Pressure on Face A   b). Pressure on Face B    c).How many times greater is the pressure on Face B?
7. The pressure inside water is measured as 250000 Pa. Find the depth of water.
Given:  (Density of water =1000 kg/m3   &     g=10 m/s2 .)
8. A hammer applies a force of 150 N on a nail tip of area 0.00003 m2. Find the pressure produced at the nail tip.
9. Oil of density 800 kg/m3 is stored in a tank up to a height of 15 m. Find the pressure exerted by the oil at the bottom of the tank. (Take g=10 m/s2).
10. A force of 80 N acts on a brake piston of area 0.004 m2. The pressure is transmitted equally to another piston of area 0.2 m2
Find:    a). Pressure created        b). Force produced on the larger piston
