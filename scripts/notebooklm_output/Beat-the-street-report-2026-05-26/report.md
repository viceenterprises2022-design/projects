### 4. Sector Analysis & Themes

**Specialty Chemicals (Oil & Gas)**
*   **Core Growth Driver:** Expansion into the USD 3.5–4 billion North American market. FCL is doubling capacity at the US-based CCT facility (80,000 MTPA).
*   **Competitive Moats:** Defensibility is underpinned by a 2-year customer onboarding cycle and weather-specific customization capabilities (operating from -20°C to 45°C), creating significant barriers for Chinese and Indian entrants.

**Healthcare & Pharma**
*   **Core Growth Driver:** The "Chronic" segment (13.3% growth) remains the primary engine. The GLP-1 (Semaglutide) market represents a massive shift, with TRP reporting ~INR 170 million in April sales. 
*   **Pipeline Catalyst:** TRP is conducting Phase III clinical trials for Resmetirom (NASH/MASH therapy), targeting a "first-to-market" status in India due to the absence of innovator presence.

**Textiles**
*   **Core Growth Driver:** A definitive shift from "conventional auxiliaries" to "value-added finishing." Growth is supported by the Amarnath plant investment and the Biotech Malaysia initiative, capturing demand from the "China+1" macro shift.

### 5. Global Intelligence Bulletin

1.  **Trade Frameworks:** Exports are benefiting from three simultaneous structural tailwinds: the India-US deal, the India-UK zero-tax FTA, and the India-EU framework.
2.  **Supply Chain Dynamics:** April FY27 logistics and freight challenges are largely stabilized. A robust "pass-through" model remains in effect, allowing firms to transfer raw material cost increases to customers.
3.  **Regulatory Cues:** Germany is accelerating innovator substitution for biosimilars. In Brazil, 58 molecules are pending ANVISA approval, and the Ozempic filing is currently under review for early generic participation.
