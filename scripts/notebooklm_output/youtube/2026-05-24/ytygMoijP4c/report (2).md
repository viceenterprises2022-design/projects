*   **Choosing Sides:** In a market defined by intense competition between entities like OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic, Karpathy’s move is interpreted as a definitive choice. By joining Anthropic, he provides a significant boost to their intellectual capital and market perception.

### 3. Anthropic’s Market Position

*   **The End of Dominance:** Karpathy’s arrival at Anthropic raises questions about the longevity of OpenAI’s market dominance, suggesting a potential rebalancing of power toward Anthropic.
