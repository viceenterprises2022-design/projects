Unified API Access — Customer FAQ

1. Access & Verification

Q1. Whose API keys are these, and can I verify usage directly in OpenAI/Anthropic/Google’s own billing dashboard under my own account?
These are proxy API keys issued and managed directly through KesarCloud's gateway routing system. Because the keys map to upstream corporate developer accounts subsidized under our corporate benefit structures, direct access to the upstream provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) billing dashboards is not possible. All telemetry, consumption logs, and billing meters are integrated and transparently visible in real-time inside the secure KesarCloud Unified Console.
Q2. What is the exact mechanism by which “tokens” are transferred - is this literally an OpenAI/Anthropic/Google enterprise agreement assigned to me, or are you acting as a reseller proxy?
KesarCloud acts as a reseller proxy and API gateway. There is no direct enterprise agreement assigned from the upstream providers to your organization. Instead, token allocation and request routing are managed by KesarCloud's proxy layer, which translates standard API payloads and forwards them to provider endpoints. This reseller structure allows us to bundle volumes and pass significant cost savings down to our clients.
Q3. Can you provide the underlying enterprise contract or reseller agreement with these three providers?
No. KesarCloud does not disclose internal partner contracts or corporate benefit allocation agreements, as these contain proprietary pricing and relationship details. Access is delivered on a white-labeled basis via our proxy endpoints, legally backed by our Master Services Agreement (MSA) and Service Level Agreements (SLAs).
Q4. Who is the legal entity behind this offer, and what jurisdiction/license do they operate under?
The service is operated by KesarCloud (kesarcloud.in). The specific legal entity details, licenses, and governing jurisdictions are disclosed in detail during the Master Services Agreement (MSA) draft phase, aligned to mutually acceptable terms with your legal team.
Q5. What happens technically when I “use” a token - am I given API keys, a dashboard, or something else?
Technically, your engineering team is provisioned with secure KesarCloud API keys and access to our Customer Dashboard. You integrate the service by simply updating the API base URL in your existing client libraries (OpenAI/Anthropic compatible SDKs) to point to KesarCloud. The dashboard provides comprehensive usage charts, request logging, cost alerts, and key management interfaces.
Q6. Can I pay only after a small test transaction is verified independently?
Yes. KesarCloud provides a complimentary trial credit of 1 Million Token Context to let your engineering team verify API compatibility, connection speeds, model output integrity, and latency before committing to a paid contract.

2. Technical Operation & Reliability

Q7. Where is the proxy hosted?
The KesarCloud proxy layer is hosted on high-availability, distributed cloud servers within secure USA-based data centers (primarily AWS and GCP). High-throughput connection tunnels ensure minimal latency overhead. Detailed hosting regions and IP ranges are available under NDA for client whitelisting.
Q8. What is the uptime SLA, and how do we monitor it?
We guarantee a monthly service uptime SLA of 99.5%. Historical and live uptime monitoring can be tracked at our Platform Status Page: kesarcloud.in/status (currently in staging and final build phase, launching Q3 2026).
Q9. What happens technically if the proxy goes down mid-request?
If a network or proxy failure occurs mid-request, our gateway will return a standard HTTP 503 Service Unavailable error accompanied by a Retry-After header. KesarCloud does not queue or buffer dropped requests. Standard client-side retry logic (e.g., exponential backoff) should be implemented on your application layer.
Q10. Are there rate limits enforced on the API keys?
Yes. To safeguard upstream accounts and ensure platform stability, KesarCloud enforces default limits of 60 Requests Per Minute (RPM) and 100,000 Tokens Per Minute (TPM) per API key. These thresholds are flexible and can be scaled according to your volume tier and contract terms.
Q11. How does the proxy handle upstream provider outages (e.g., OpenAI or Anthropic downtime)?
We run automated health checks on all upstream providers every 30 seconds. If an upstream provider suffers an outage, the KesarCloud gateway can automatically failover and route requests to equivalent models from alternative providers (e.g., routing from Claude to Gemini) based on failover preferences configured in your dashboard.

3. Billing & Pricing

Q12. How is usage metered?
Usage is metered exactly at the token level returned by the upstream provider's API headers (input tokens + output tokens). KesarCloud does not inject any metering overhead, markups, or artificial latency.
Q13. Do unused tokens roll over?
Standard token blocks are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. For custom enterprise agreements, custom rollover parameters can be structured in the MSA.
Q14. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, ACH, and corporate wire transfers. For international clients, settlement in USDT/USDC is preferred for faster clearance.
Q15. Are there volume discounts?
Yes. Tiered volume discounts are available starting at monthly commitments of 10M, 100M, and 1B tokens. Please refer to Appendix B for standard catalog rates or request a custom quote from our sales team.

4. Security & Compliance

Q16. Do you log prompts and response completions?
No. We log only transaction metadata (token counts, model identifiers, timestamps, and error codes) for billing and rate-limiting purposes. Prompts and responses are processed transiently in-memory and are never written to disk. A Zero-Retention policy is standard for all enterprise plans.
Q17. Is data sent to upstream providers subject to their training policies?
Yes. Because requests are forwarded to the official enterprise APIs of OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, they are governed by their respective developer terms. These terms explicitly state that data submitted through API endpoints is not used for model training.
Q18. Do you offer Single Sign-On (SSO) or SAML integration?
SSO and SAML integration for the customer dashboard is on our product roadmap for Q4 2026. Currently, dashboard access is secured via email verification and token-based credentials.
