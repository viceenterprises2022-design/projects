Grade 7 Biomes – Important Terms &
Definitions
1. Biome
A large region with a specific climate, plants, and animals.
2. Ecosystem
Living and non-living things interacting in an area.
3. Environment
The surroundings where living things exist.
4. Biodiversity
The variety of living things in one place.
5. Habitat
The natural home of a plant or animal.
6. Species
A group of living things that can reproduce together.
7. Population
All members of one species in an area.
8. Community
Different populations living together.
9. Adaptation
A feature or behavior that helps survival.
10. Extinction
When a species no longer exists.
11. Climate
The usual weather of a place over many years.
12. Weather
Daily conditions of the atmosphere.
13. Temperature
How hot or cold a place is.
14. Precipitation
Rain, snow, or hail that falls.
15. Humidity
Amount of water vapor in air.
16. Drought
A long period with little or no rain.
17. Climate Change
Long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns.
🌿 Major Biomes
18. Grassland
Land dominated by grasses with few trees.
19. Desert
Very dry area with little rainfall.
20. Tropical Rainforest
Hot, wet forest near the equator.
21. Tundra
Very cold biome with frozen soil.
22. Taiga (Boreal Forest)
Cold forest with coniferous trees.
23. Savanna
Tropical grassland with scattered trees.
24. Temperate Forest
Forest with four seasons and moderate rainfall.
25. Producer
Plant that makes its own food.
26. Consumer
Animal that eats plants or animals.
27. Decomposer
Organism that breaks down dead matter.
28. Herbivore
Animal that eats plants.
29. Carnivore
Animal that eats meat.
30. Omnivore
Animal that eats plants and animals.
31. Food Chain
Shows how energy moves between organisms.
32. Food Web
Many connected food chains.
33. Soil
Top layer of earth where plants grow.
34. Fertile Soil
Soil good for growing plants.
35. Erosion
Wearing away of soil and land.
36. Desertification
Land turning into desert.
37. Permafrost
Permanently frozen ground.
38. Deforestation
Cutting down forests.
39. Pollution
Harmful substances in environment.
40. Overgrazing
Animals eating too many plants.
41. Urbanization
Growth of cities.
42. Agriculture
Farming and growing crops.
43. Habitat Loss
Destruction of natural homes.
44. Global Warming
Increase in Earth's temperature.
45. Conservation
Protecting nature and resources.
46. Sustainability
Using resources wisely for the future.
47. National Park
Protected natural area.
48. Wildlife Sanctuary
Safe place for animals.
49. Reforestation
Planting trees again.
50. Renewable Resource
Resource that can be replaced naturally.
