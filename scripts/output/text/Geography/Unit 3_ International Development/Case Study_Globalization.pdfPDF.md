Case Study 1: Apple iPhone Production – A Global Supply
Chain
Overview:
The iPhone may be designed in the USA, but it’s made by people and parts from all over the world!
This is an example of economic globalization – where production and business are connected across
different countries.
Key Points:
•  Design & Software: Developed in California, USA.
•  Raw Materials:
o  Cobalt from Congo (used in batteries).
o  Rare earth metals from China.
•  Parts Manufacturing:
o  Display screen made in South Korea (Samsung) and Japan.
o  Chips made in Taiwan.
o  Camera parts from Germany.
•  Assembly: Done mainly in China (by companies like Foxconn).
•  Shipping: iPhones are then shipped to countries all over the world for sale.
Case Study 2: Bollywood’s International Popularity
Overview:
Bollywood is the name of the Indian film industry based in Mumbai. Today, Bollywood movies are
watched by millions of people around the world, showing how culture spreads globally.
Key Points:
•  Popular Countries for Bollywood:
o  Middle East – large Indian diaspora and love for Indian music.
o  United Kingdom & Canada – Indian communities and increasing local fans.
o  Africa & Southeast Asia – Bollywood movies are shown on TV and in cinemas.
o  Even  non-Indian  audiences  in  Germany,  Russia,  Japan  enjoy  Indian  dance  and
drama.
•  Why it became popular:
o  Emotional and family-based stories.
o  Dance and music appeal to a wide audience.
o  Availability on YouTube, Netflix, Amazon Prime.
o
Indian diaspora sharing movies and culture.
