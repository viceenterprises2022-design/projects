The Story of Novera and Solara
A long time ago, two countries—Novera and Solara—started their journey at almost the

But as the years passed, things began to change.
In Solara, children walked into bright classrooms every morning. Hospitals were well-

In Novera, Rohan had to walk long distances to reach school. Sometimes, his classes
were interrupted due to lack of resources. His family found it harder to access medical
help when they needed it.
Building and Connecting
As Aarav imagined both countries, he noticed something else.

But in Solara, these were carefully managed. Clean parks and protected areas were

In Novera, some resources were overused, and certain areas showed signs of damage.
Looking Beyond Borders
Solara connected with other countries through trade and new ideas. People exchanged
goods, knowledge, and technology.
Novera interacted less with the outside world, and changes seemed slower.
