1. Religious Faith
Belief in serving God’s will
Desire to reach the Holy Land
Hope of forgiveness of sins
2. Adventure
Excitement of travel to new lands
Chance to be part of something big
Opportunity for honor and glory
3. Escape from Hardship
Many were poor peasants with little land
Chance to leave behind hunger and poverty
Hope of a better life
4. Desire for Wealth
Belief in gaining riches from the East
Possibility of land and treasures
Chance for social advancement
