GOAL 1: To define and interpret the concept of a balanced diet and its
importance for health
Hidden Sugars – Are Healthy Foods Always Healthy?
Label Analysis Challenge
·         Provide 4–6 food wrappers/labels (e.g., flavored yogurt, fruit juices, protein bars, breakfast
cereals).
·         Students work in pairs to:
Ø  Locate total sugars and added sugars
Ø  Compare with WHO sugar recommendations
Ø  Rank the items from least to most misleading
Marketing vs. Reality Board:
Students are given or select one item from above and create a 2-column
chart/poster:
o   Column 1: What the marketing/packaging says (e.g., "No added sugar", "Fitness
food")
o   Column 2: What the label reveals (e.g., 28g sugar per serving, low protein)
Student Reflection :
·         Prompt: “Would you still eat this product? Why or why not?”
·         Encourage connection to self-awareness and responsible decision-making
Peer Gallery Walk:
·         Students post their charts and circulate to compare insights with peers.
