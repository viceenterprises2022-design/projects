Deficiency Diseases / Conditions
1.  Carbohydrates
○  Condition: Fatigue, weakness, and weight loss
○  Reason: Carbohydrates are the main energy source; deficiency leads to low
energy and poor growth.
○  Note: Severe carbohydrate deficiency is rare, but can cause ketosis (body starts
breaking fats for energy).
2.  Proteins
○  Condition: Kwashiorkor (swelling, stunted growth, skin/hair changes)
○  Condition: Marasmus (severe wasting, very thin body, muscle loss)
○  Reason: Proteins are essential for growth, repair, and enzymes.
3.  Fats
○  Condition: Dry skin, poor temperature regulation, vitamin deficiencies (A, D,
E, K need fat to be absorbed)
○  Reason: Fats provide energy storage, insulation, and help absorb fat-soluble
vitamins.
4.  Dietary Fiber
○  Condition: Constipation and digestive problems
○  Long-term risk: Obesity, diabetes, heart disease due to poor digestion and
blood sugar imbalance.
○  Reason: Fiber helps bowel movement, regulates blood sugar, and maintains gut
health.
