Types Of Biomolecules
Food is a source of large molecules that are needed for life called
biomolecules. Biomolecules are the molecules that are produced by the
living organisms.
There are four major biomolecules that make up. In general, they are
called macromolecules (aka organic molecules aka biomolecules).

Polymers are built by joining small subunits called monomers together.
