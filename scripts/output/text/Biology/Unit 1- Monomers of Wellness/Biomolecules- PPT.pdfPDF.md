Nucleic Acid
A major function of nucleic acids involves the storage and expression of genomic information.
There are two main types of nucleic acid:
DNA- Deoxyribonucleic Acid
RNA- Ribonucleic Acid
The monomers of DNA and RNA are called Nucleotides.
The elements found in nucleotides are carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen and phosphorus.
