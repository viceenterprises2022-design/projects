What is osmosis and diffusion?
Diffusion: is the movement of particles from a region of
high concentration to a region of low concentration
Osmosis: is the movement of water from an area of
high concentration to an area of low concentration across
a partially permeable membrane
Partially permeable membrane: it is a membrane that
has tiny pores in it which allow small molecules to pass
through but not large molecules, an example of this is
cell membrane

Water Potential
Water potential is
the concentration of
water molecules
Osmosis in Plant cells
Plant cell placed in solution
with less water* (hypertonic
solution*)→ water moves out
of the cell → the cytoplasm
pulls away from the cell wall.
The cell becomes flaccid
(* Low water potential i.e. concentration
of water molecules is less)
(*Hypertonic solution – more solutes and
less solvent).
Osmosis in Plant cells
Plant cell placed in solution
with lots of water* (hypotonic
solution*) → water enters the
cell → Cytoplasm swells and
pushes against the cell wall
Cell becomes turgid
(* High water potential)
(*Hypotonic solution – less solutes and
more solvent).
Turgor Pressure in Plant cells
Pressure exerted on the cell wall as the
cytoplasm swells is called turgor pressure. Helps
the plant to stay upright.
Water loss > water uptake → plant wilts
