Grade 7 Worksheets for practice | Topic: Writing a speech
Explainer: When we write a speech, we understand we have to put three important parts
together – 1) Introduction 2) Body 3) Conclusion.
1)  The introduction should have the below elements:
-  A good hook. It’s a line or a saying or a quote from a famous personality, that is
sure to catch the attention of the audience and make them listen to you.
-  Credentials. This is where you introduce yourself as a speaker and explain why
your audience should listen to you. Here, as per the relevance of the topic you’re
speaking about, you could mention your background, your achievements, your
challenges, or even your culture.
-  Opening statement. This is a statement you make to clearly communicate your
topic, its importance to you or the listener, and your main view of the topic.
2)  The Body should present at least 3 statements that elaborate your opening
statement and provide reasons for each. Ensure that your statements are closely
related to one another and that they’re not off topic. When you provide reasons
in support of your statement, you should also examine your statement critically.
This means you talk about ideas that may be used to question your opening
statement and your reasons. It helps to clarify your statement further and shows
your listener that you have considered a variety of opinions, especially those that
may challenge you.
3)  The conclusion:
-  Round up your speech by reiterating your main point and your key reasons, thank
the listener for their time and attention. Provide a call to action.
TASK.
Based on the above explainer, write a speech about a moment or an event that
happened during the current academic year, that may have changed the way you look at
your self, your life, or some of your choices. Write around 500 words and follow the
structure described above. Give a creative title of your choice that inspires others.
