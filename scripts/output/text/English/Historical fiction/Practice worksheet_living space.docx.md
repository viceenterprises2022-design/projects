Question 1: The poem ‘Living Space’ describes a house that is unstable but still standing. Analyse how the poet presents both danger and hope in the poem.
How to answer:
Step 1: Find one detail that shows danger.
Step 2: Find one detail that shows hope or faith.
Step 3: Explain what each detail suggests.
Step 4: Connect them to show how they work together.
Question 2: Discuss how the poem moves from describing the structure of the house to describing the eggs. Why is this order important?
How to Answer
Step 1: Describe what the first part of the poem focuses on.
Step 2: Describe what changes in the second part.
Step 3: Explain why the poet may have structured it this way.
Question 3: Create a short paragraph from the perspective of someone living inside this house.
How to Answer
Step 1: Imagine the setting (unstable beams, small space).
Step 2: Describe how it feels physically.
Step 3: Include one emotion (fear, hope, pride).
Step 4: Keep it realistic.
Question 4: Comment on the effect of the word “miraculous” in the line “The whole structure leans dangerously towards the miraculous.”
How to Answer
Step 1: Identify what ‘miraculous’ means.
Step 2: Explain why this word is surprising.
Step 3: Describe its effect on the reader.
