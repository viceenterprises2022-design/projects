SOI - EXPLANATION
Identity is dynamic, it is ALWAYS changing.
Our environment influences and impacts our sense of identity.
How we choose to express ourselves is determined by many
factors – society, educational background, personal beliefs,
values, etc.
Society and its norms makes us CONFORM.
We may change our identity based on how people view us.
Our idea of self, and identity changes with change in
environment.
