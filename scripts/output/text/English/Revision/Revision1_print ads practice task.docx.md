Study this ad poster
Criterion A – Analysing
Question: Look closely at the McDonald’s advertisement.
Which part of the rhetorical triangle (ethos, pathos, logos) do you think the ad uses the most? Explain your answer in one simple sentence.
Criterion B – Organising
Question: Using bullet points, list two features you notice in the McDonald’s ad (for example: colours, slogan, picture of food, characters). Then write one line explaining how each feature helps the ad persuade people.
Criterion C – Producing Text
Question: Write a short 3–4 line mini-ad for McDonald’s, inspired by the poster.
Use one technique from the explainer (fear appeal, tall claim, bandwagon, or testimonial). Keep your writing simple and clear.
Criterion D – Using Language
Question: Rewrite this weak slogan to make it more persuasive, like a real McDonald’s ad: “The burger is nice. You should eat it.”
Use stronger words that are easy to understand.
