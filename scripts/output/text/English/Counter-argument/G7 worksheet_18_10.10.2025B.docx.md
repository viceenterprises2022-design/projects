Warm-up
What is an Argument?
An argument is a reasoned statement that presents a point of view supported by evidence. A counter-argument presents the opposite side to show that you understand other perspectives.
Example:
Argument: School uniforms create equality among students.
Counter-Argument: Some may argue that uniforms limit students’ freedom of expression.
Refutation: However, freedom of expression can still be shown through creativity in classwork and behaviour, not only clothing.
