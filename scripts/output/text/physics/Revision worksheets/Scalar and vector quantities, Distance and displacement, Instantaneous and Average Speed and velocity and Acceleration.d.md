Grade 7 Physics
Topics: Scalar and vector quantities, Distance and displacement, Instantaneous and Average Speed and velocity and Acceleration
Section A: Conceptual — 6 questions
Define scalar and vector quantities. Give one example of each.
Is mass a scalar or vector quantity? Justify.
Which of these is a vector: speed, acceleration, temperature, force?
State the difference between distance and displacement with an example.
Can the displacement be zero even when distance is not? Explain.
A person walks 5 m north and then 5 m south. What is the distance and what is the displacement?
Section B: Numerical & Application — 8 questions
A car travels 60 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed?
A person walks 3 km east, then 4 km north. Find the total distance and displacement.
A bike moves with speeds of 30 km/h for the first hour and 60 km/h for the next hour. What is the average speed?
A train covers 300 m in 20 seconds. What is its speed in m/s?
A ball falls from a height with an initial velocity of 0 m/s and reaches 20 m/s in 4 seconds. Calculate the acceleration.
A car starts from rest and reaches a velocity of 40 m/s in 5 seconds. What is its acceleration?
A runner completes a 400 m lap in 50 seconds. If the track is circular, what is the displacement and average velocity?
Calculate the instantaneous speed of a car if it covers 10 meters in 0.5 seconds at that moment.
Section C: Situational Reasoning — 3 questions
Can a body have a constant speed but changing velocity? Give one example.
A car moves in a circular path at a constant speed. Is it accelerating? Why or why not?
In which of the following cases is the acceleration zero:
a) A parked car
b) A car moving with constant speed in a straight line
c) A car taking a turn
d) A car speeding up
Section D: Mixed Challenge Problems — 3 questions
A cyclist covers 100 m north, 50 m east, then 100 m south. Calculate:
a) Total distance
b) Displacement
c) Average speed if time taken is 30 seconds
d) Average velocity
A ball is thrown vertically upward with a speed of 20 m/s. It reaches maximum height in 2 seconds.
a) What is the acceleration?
b) What is the velocity at the topmost point?
A student runs a 100 m race in 12.5 seconds. Her speed keeps changing throughout.
a) What is her average speed?
b) Why can't we determine instantaneous speed from this data?
