Area of Irregular Shapes
In real life, not all shapes are perfect squares or circles. Some shapes are irregular – they don’t have equal sides or regular angles. For example, a pond, a park, or even a plot of land might have a random shape. Finding the area of such shapes is still possible using some smart methods.
Methods to Find Area of Irregular Shapes
1. Divide the shape into known shapes (like rectangles, triangles, or semicircles).
2. Find the area of each part.
3. Add all the areas together to get the total area.
Example:
Imagine an L-shaped garden. You can divide it into two rectangles.
Find the area of each rectangle using the formula: Area = Length × Width.
Then add both areas together to get the total area of the garden.
Practice Questions:
1. A plot of land is shaped like an L. The horizontal part is 8 m by 4 m and the vertical part is 6 m by 3 m. What is the total area?
2. A swimming pool is shaped like a rectangle (10 m × 4 m) with a semicircle at one end (radius 2 m). Find the total area. (Use π ≈ 3.14)
3. Divide a star-shaped park into triangles. Each triangle is about 6 m². If there are 5 such triangles, what is the total area?
Note: Always write units in your final answer!
