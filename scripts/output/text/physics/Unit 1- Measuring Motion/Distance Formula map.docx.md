Distance Formula Decision Guide
Case A: Uniform speed (no acceleration) eg: A car cruising on a highway at a constant speed of 60km/h, An escalator moving people upward, a ceiling fan at steady rotation, a conveyor belt in the airport, Earth's rotation
Distance = Speed × Time
Case B: Uniform acceleration eg: A toy car rolling down a inclined ramp, free fall of an object, a car starting from rest (initial velocity=0) with a steady press on accelerator, a train leaving a station, a ceiling fan blade slowing down
Distance = ut + ½at²
