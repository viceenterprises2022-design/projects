Marking Scheme – Grade 7 Physics Formative Assessment
Section A – Criterion A: Knowing & Understanding
Q1. Fundamental vs Derived Quantities
Fundamental quantity: cannot be defined in terms of others, e.g. length, mass, time (½ mark for definition, ½ mark each for 2 examples).
Velocity = displacement ÷ time → depends on length & time → derived.
Q2. Unit Conversion
5.6 km=5600 m
4500 cm3=4.5 L
Q3. A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 2 m/s2 for 5 seconds.
(a) What is its final velocity?
(b) What distance does it cover in this time?
Marking Scheme:
(a) Use v=u+at  v=0+(2×5)=10 m/s
(b) Use s=ut+1/2at2 ; s=0+12×2×25=25 m.
Q4. A motorbike is moving at an initial velocity of 5 m/s. It accelerates uniformly at 1.5 m/s2 for 8 seconds.
(a) What is its final velocity?
(b) How far does it travel in this time?
Marking Scheme:
(a) v=u+at=5+(1.5×8)=17 m/s
(b) s=ut+1/2at2=(5×8)+12(1.5)(64) s=40+48=88 m.
Q5. Motion Concepts
Distance: total path length (scalar); Displacement: shortest straight-line distance from start to end (vector).
Instantaneous speed: speed at a particular instant; Average speed: total distance ÷ total time.
Section B – Criterion B: Inquiring & Designing
Q6. Area, Volume & Density
Volume = 10×6×4=240 cm3
Density = Mass ÷ Volume = 480÷240=2.0 g/cm3.
Real-life application: designing ships, identifying materials, construction, etc. (any valid example).
Q7. Scientific Method
Hypothesis: “If the ball is dropped from a greater height, then it will take longer to reach the ground.”
Controlled variable: type of ball, air resistance, surface, measuring method.
Q8. Speed, Velocity & Acceleration
Total distance = 200 + 150 = 350 m.
Displacement = 200 – 150 = 50 m north.
Average speed = total distance ÷ total time = 350÷70=5.0 m/s
Average velocity = displacement ÷ total time = 50÷70≈0.71 m/s north
