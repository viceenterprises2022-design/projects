SDL on Mechanical Advantage and Efficiency of Levers
What is a Lever?
A lever is a simple machine made of a rigid bar that can rotate around a fulcrum (pivot point).
Levers help us lift or move loads with less effort by amplifying or changing the direction of force.
Terms Related to a Lever
Types of Levers
Levers are classified based on the relative positions of the Fulcrum (F), Load (L), and Effort (E).
Levers and Mechanical Advantage
MA > 1 → lever multiplies force (lifts heavy loads).
MA < 1 → lever multiplies speed or distance.
MA = 1 → lever only changes direction of force.
