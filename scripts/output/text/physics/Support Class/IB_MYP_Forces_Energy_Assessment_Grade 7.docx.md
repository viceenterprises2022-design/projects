Criterion B Case Study: Pressure in Real Life
Case Study: Why Do Snowshoes Work Better Than Regular Shoes?
During a winter expedition in the Himalayan region, a group of trekkers noticed something interesting.
One of the trekkers, Arjun, wore regular hiking boots and found himself sinking deeply into the snow with every step. This made walking extremely tiring and slow.
Another trekker, Meera, used specialized snowshoes—wide, flat footwear designed for walking on snow. Surprisingly, she was able to walk more easily and did not sink as much into the snow.
Curious about this difference, the group began discussing possible reasons. Some thought it was due to weight differences, while others believed it had something to do with the area of contact between the shoe and the snow.
To investigate this scientifically, the group decided to design an experiment that could help them understand what factor affects how deeply an object sinks into a soft surface like snow.
