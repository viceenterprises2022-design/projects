Questions on Universe and Celestial Bodies
Recall and Understanding

Formation of atoms
Formation of galaxies
Big Bang
Formation of the solar system
Differentiate between the Geocentric Model and the Heliocentric Model.
Why was the heliocentric model accepted over the geocentric model?
Name the eight planets of the Solar System in order from the Sun.
Conceptual Questions
Why are inner planets different from outer planets in size and composition?
Explain why the Asteroid Belt did not form into a planet.
What is a Galaxy? Name the galaxy in which our solar system is located.
Compare spiral, elliptical, and irregular galaxies.
Why do eclipses not occur every month even though the Moon revolves around Earth every month?
