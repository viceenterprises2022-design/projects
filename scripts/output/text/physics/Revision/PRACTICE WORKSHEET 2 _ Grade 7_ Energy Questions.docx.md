PRACTICE WORKSHEET: 1
Grade 7
Topic: kinetic Energy, Potential Energy and Laws of conservation of Energy
A football of mass 0.5 kg is kicked with a velocity of 12 m/s. Calculate its kinetic energy.
A bucket of mass 8 kg is lifted to a height of 6 m. Find its gravitational potential energy.
(Take g=10 m/s2).
A car of mass 1500 kg is travelling at 25 m/s. Calculate its kinetic energy.
A 5 kg object has 250 J of potential energy. Find the height at which it is placed.
(Take g=10 m/s2)
A runner of mass 60 kg has a kinetic energy of 750 J. Calculate the speed of the runner.
A coconut of mass 2 kg falls from a tree 15 m high. Calculate:
a) Potential energy at the top
b) Kinetic energy just before it hits the ground
(Take g=10 m/s2)
A bike and rider together have a mass of 200 kg and move with a speed of 8 m/s. Find the kinetic energy.
A 20 kg suitcase is kept on a platform 3.5 m above the ground. Calculate its gravitational potential energy.
(Take g=9.8 m/s2)
A 2 kg ball is dropped from a height of 10 m. Calculate:
a) Its potential energy at the top
b) Its kinetic energy just before it hits the ground
(Take g=10 m/s2)
A roller coaster car of mass 500 kg is at the top of a track 20 m high. Assuming no energy is lost, calculate its kinetic energy at the bottom.
(Take g=10 m/s2)
A 1.5 kg stone is thrown upward with a kinetic energy of 300 J. Find the maximum height reached by the stone.
(Take g=10 m/s2)
A pendulum bob has 50 J of potential energy at its highest point. If total mechanical energy remains constant, calculate:
a) Its kinetic energy at the highest point
b) Its kinetic energy at the lowest point
