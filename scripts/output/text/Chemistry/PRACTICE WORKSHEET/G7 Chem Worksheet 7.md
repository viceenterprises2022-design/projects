Practice Worksheet – Criterion B (Inquiring
& Designing)
Topics: Mixtures, Colloids, and Suspensions
Grade 7 – Chemistry
Task 1: Understanding the Problem
You are given three different substances in the science lab:
•  A mixture of sand and water
•  A suspension of chalk powder in water
•  A colloid of milk
Your goal is to design investigations to separate or study these mixtures.
Q1. Formulate Research Questions (RQ)
Write clear research questions in the format:
“How does the independent variable (IV) affect the dependent variable (DV)?”
a. For the sand-water mixture
b. For the chalk suspension
c. For the milk colloid
Q2. Hypothesis
For any one of the above research questions, write a suitable hypothesis.
Example: If …, then … because …
Q3. Identifying Variables
Choose the investigation on chalk suspension. Identify:
•
Independent Variable (IV)
•  Dependent Variable (DV)
•  At least two Controlled Variables (CVs)

Q4. Materials and Apparatus
List the materials and apparatus needed to test your hypothesis for the chosen mixture.
Q5. Method / Procedure
Write a step-by-step method so that another student group could follow it to repeat your
investigation.
Q6. Safety Precautions
State two safety precautions you will follow while conducting this investigation.

Q7. Experimental Setup Diagram
