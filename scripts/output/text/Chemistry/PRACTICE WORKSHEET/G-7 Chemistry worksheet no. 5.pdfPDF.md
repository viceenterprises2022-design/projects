Worksheet No. 5
Grade 7 – Scientific Inquiry Worksheet (Elements, Compounds, and
Mixtures)
Instructions: For each scenario below:
1.  Frame a Research Question (RQ) in the format:
a.  “How does [IV] affect [DV]?”
2.  Write a Hypothesis in the format:
a.  “If [IV], then [DV], because [scientific reasoning].”
3.  Identify Variables:
a.  Independent Variable (IV): The factor you change
b.  Dependent Variable (DV): The factor you measure
c.  Control Variables (CVs): The factors you keep the same
Scenario 1: Salt in Water
A student stirs different amounts of salt into glasses of water and notices the taste
changes.
