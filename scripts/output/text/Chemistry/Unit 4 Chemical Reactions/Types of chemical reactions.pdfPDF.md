Types of chemical Reactions
1. Synthesis (Combination) Reaction
A synthesis reaction is a reaction in which two or more substances combine to form one
new substance.
Key idea: Many → One
Example: When iron reacts with sulfur, a new compound iron sulfide is formed.
Word Equation:       Iron + Sulphur → Iron sulphide
Balanced Equation:  Fe + S → FeS
2. Decomposition Reaction
A decomposition reaction is a reaction in which one compound breaks down into two or
more simpler substances.
Key idea: One → Many
(Usually needs heat, light, or electricity)
Example:
Calcium carbonate breaks down when heated to form Calcium oxide and carbondioxide
Word Equation:       Calcium carbonate → Calcium Oxide + Carbon dioxide
Balanced Equation:                CaCO₃ →  CaO + CO₂
3. Single Displacement Reaction
A single displacement reaction occurs when one element replaces another element in a
compound.
Key idea: One element replaces another. The more reactive metal displaces the less reactive
metal from its salt solution.
Example:
Zinc replaces copper from copper sulfate solution to form Zinc sulphate and copper
Word Equation: Zinc + copper sulfate → Zinc sulphate + copper
Balanced Equation:Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
4. Double Displacement Reaction
A double displacement reaction occurs when two compounds exchange ions to form two
new compounds.
Key idea: Exchange of partners
Example:
Sodium chloride reacts with silver nitrate to form solid silver chloride(white precipitate) and
sodium nitrate
Word Equation: Sodium Chloride + Silver Nitrate → Silver chloride + sodium nitrate
Balanced Equation: NaCl + AgNO₃ → AgCl + NaNO₃
5. Combustion Reaction
A combustion reaction occurs when a substance reacts with oxygen, producing heat and
light.
Key idea: Fuel + Oxygen → Energy
Example:
Burning magnesium ribbon in air forms magnesium oxide producing dazzling white light
with heat
Word Equation: Magnesium + Oxygen → Magnesium Oxide + heat
Balanced Equation: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO
6. Exothermic Reaction
An exothermic reaction is a reaction that releases heat energy. Heat is released to the
surroundings, making the reaction feel warm.
Example:
Combustion of methane produces carbon dioxide and water
Word Equation: Methane + Oxygen → carbon dioxide + water+ heat
Balanced Equation: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O + energy
7. Endothermic Reaction
An endothermic reaction is a reaction that absorbs heat energy from its surroundings. Heat
is needed for the reaction to occur.
Example:Thermal decomposition of calcium carbonate. Calcium carbonate decomposes on
heating to form calcium oxide and carbon dioxide
Word Equation: Calcium carbonate + heat → Calcium oxide + carbon dioxide
Balanced Equation: CaCO₃ + heat → CaO + CO₂
