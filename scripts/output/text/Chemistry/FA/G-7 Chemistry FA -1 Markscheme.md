Grade 7 Chemistry FA-1 (Criteria A & B)
Criterion A: Knowing & Understanding
Q1. Multiple Choice Questions                                                                        ( Level 1–2)
a) Correct answer: B. Copper → Element
b) Correct answer: C. They have a fixed composition.
Q2. Cutting Aluminium foil into tiny pieces turns it into a compound. Explain
why it is incorrect?                                                                                                   (Level 3–4)
Cutting Aluminium foil into tiny pieces does not turn it into a compound because it
is made of only one type of atom—Aluminium—so it remains an element. Cutting
only changes the size and shape; no new substance is formed. Each small piece
still consists of the same Aluminium atoms, so no compound is created.
Q3. Distinguish between a mixture and a compound by comparing air with
water (H₂O). Give two differences.                                                               (Level 3–4)
Composition: Mixture (air) has variable composition; Compound (H₂O) has fixed
composition.
Separation: Mixtures can be separated physically; Compounds need chemical methods.
Properties: Substances in a mixture retain properties; In a compound, properties are
different from elements.
Q4. Silver solid forming black layer (Classify) (Level 1–2)
Silver solid = Element
Black layer (silver oxide) = Compound
Criterion B: Inquiring & Designing
a. Research Question (Level 1-2)
“How does the length (or mass) of magnesium ribbon affect the mass of magnesium oxide
formed?”
b. Hypothesis with Explanation (Level 3–4)
Hypothesis:
If the length of magnesium ribbon is increased, then the mass of magnesium oxide formed
will also increase, because more magnesium will be available to react with oxygen to
produce more ash (magnesium oxide).
c. Variables (Level 3–4)
Independent Variable (IV): Length (or mass) of magnesium ribbon.
Dependent Variable (DV): Mass of magnesium oxide formed.
Controlled Variables (CV): Type of flame, crucible, lid, balance, same type of
ribbon, environment.
d. Safety Precaution (Level 1–2)
Any one valid precaution:
Wear safety goggles to protect your eyes from bright light and ash.
Use tongs to hold hot crucible.
Avoid looking directly at burning magnesium.
