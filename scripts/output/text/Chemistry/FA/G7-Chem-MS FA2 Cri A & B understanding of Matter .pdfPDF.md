Criteria A:  Knowing & Understanding
Strand (i)
1.  Define:                                                                                                                        Level 1-2
a.  Colloids: A colloid is a type of heterogeneous mixture.The particles, ranging in
size between those of a true solution and a suspension, are large enough to scatter
light but small enough that they do not settle under gravity. (e.g., milk).
b.  Suspension:   A heterogeneous mixture in which large particles are dispersed in a
liquid or gas but settle standing (e.g., sand in water).

Strand (iv)
c.  Design a step-by-step method that you would follow in the lab to separate the
mixture into pure salt, dry sand, and clear water.                                        Level 7-8
•  Collect the required materials and apparatus:
•  Place filter paper in the funnel and filter the mixture into a beaker to separate sand
from salt solution.
•  Wash the sand with a little distilled water to remove any salt, then dry it in an
oven/sunlight.
•  Collect the filtrate (salt solution) in a clean beaker.
•  Heat the solution in an evaporating dish until all the water evaporates.
•  Collect dry salt crystals.
•
(Optional extension) Condense the evaporated water using a condenser/distillation
setup and collect it for reuse.
