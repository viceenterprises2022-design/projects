Grade 7 Chemistry
UNIT – Periodic Table
Summative Assessment & Project
Periodic Table at Home – Elements Around Us
Key Concept: Relationships
Related Concepts: Patterns, Structure
Global Context: Scientific and Technical Innovation
Statement of Inquiry (SOI):
The relationships between an element’s position, electronic arrangement, and properties help us understand its uses in everyday life.
Activity Objective
You will explore elements of the periodic table by connecting them to real-life objects at home, building simple models, and expressing your understanding creatively through a hands-on investigation and presentation.
Parts of the Assignment
Part 1: Research
Choose ONE element from the periodic table
(Preferably from Group 1, Group 7, or Transition metals)
Write the following in your own words:
Name and symbol of the element
Group and period
Type of element (metal / non-metal / transition metal)
Explain:
Electronic arrangement (up to Calcium)
Number of valence electrons
How the position of the element relates to its properties
Identify at least two real-life uses of your element
Part 2: Hands-On Investigation & Creative Presentation
You will explore your chosen element by collecting real-life objects and presenting your understanding through a creative format.
