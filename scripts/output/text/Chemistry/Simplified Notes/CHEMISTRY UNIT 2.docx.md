CHEMISTRY UNIT 2: AMBIGUOUS PARTICLES
Composition of Atoms & Bohr's Model
PART 1: COMPOSITION OF ATOMS
What is Inside an Atom?
An atom has two main parts:
NUCLEUS (Center)
Contains protons and neutrons
Very dense and heavy
Takes up very little space
ELECTRONS (Outside)
Move around the nucleus
Much lighter than protons and neutrons
Move in shells/energy levels

REMEMBER:

Neutrons = NEUTRAL (no charge)

IMPORTANT NUMBERS
Atomic Number = Number of protons
This tells you what element it is
Hydrogen = 1 proton
Carbon = 6 protons
Oxygen = 8 protons
Mass Number = Protons + Neutrons
This tells you the total mass
Formula: Mass Number = Protons + Neutrons
CALCULATING PARTICLES
How to find the number of each particle:
Step 1: Find Protons
Protons = Atomic Number
Step 2: Find Neutrons
Neutrons = Mass Number - Atomic Number
Step 3: Find Electrons
Electrons = Protons (in a neutral atom)

PART 2: BOHR'S MODEL OF THE ATOM
What is Bohr's Model?
A model that shows electrons arranged in shells around the nucleus.
Think of it like: Planets orbiting the sun
Nucleus = Sun (center)
Electrons = Planets (moving in circles around center)
ELECTRON SHELLS (Energy Levels)
Electrons are arranged in shells around the nucleus.
Rules:
Electrons fill the closest shell first
Each shell can hold a certain number of electrons
Electrons stay in their shells (they don't fall into the nucleus)
