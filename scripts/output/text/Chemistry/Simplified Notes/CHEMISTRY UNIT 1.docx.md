TOPIC 2: SEPARATION TECHNIQUES
Why separate mixtures?
To get pure substances
To remove unwanted materials
Main Separation Methods:
Filtration
Separates solids from liquids
Uses paper or cloth with holes
Example: Separating sand from water
Distillation
Separates liquids from liquids
Uses heat and cooling
Example: Getting pure water from salt water
Crystallization
Gets solid crystals from solutions
Uses cooling or evaporation
Example: Making rock salt crystals
Fractional Distillation
Separates crude oil into different products
Uses heat (more complex distillation)
Example: Getting petrol, diesel, fuel oil

TOPIC 4: PAPER CHROMATOGRAPHY
What is it?
A way to separate colored dyes in a liquid
Shows which colors are mixed together
How it Works:
Put a drop of colored liquid on paper
Stand the paper in water
Water moves up the paper
Different colors separate and move different distances
Rf Value (Important!):
Measures how far a color travels
Formula: Rf = Distance traveled by color / Distance traveled by water
Rf value is between 0 and 1
Each color has its own Rf value
Why Use It?
Find out what dyes are in a mixture
Check if two colors are the same
Identify unknown substances
