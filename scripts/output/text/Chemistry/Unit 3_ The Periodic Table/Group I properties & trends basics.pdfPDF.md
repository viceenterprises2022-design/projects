Group I properties & trends: basics
Imagine a metal you can cut with a butter knife or one that explodes just by touching
water. Welcome to the world of Alkali Metals! These elements live in Group 1 (the very
first column) of the periodic table.
Who’s in the Family?
The main members of this group are:
•  Lithium (Li): The lightest of them all.
•  Sodium (Na): Found in your table salt, but as a pure metal, it’s a whole different
story!.
•  Potassium (K): Vital for your heart and found in bananas.
•  Rubidium (Rb), Cesium (Cs), and Francium (Fr):
•  The Group 1 metals are also called the alkali metals as they form alkaline
solutions when reacted with water
•  They all contain just one electron in their outer shell
Physical properties of the Group 1 metals
•  The Group 1 metals:
o  Are soft and easy to cut, getting even softer and generally denser as you
move down the Group (sodium and potassium do not follow the trend in
density)
o  Have shiny silvery surfaces when freshly cut
o  Conduct heat and electricity
o  They all have low melting points and low densities compared to other
metals, and the melting point generally decreases as you move down the
Group; some would melt on a hot day
