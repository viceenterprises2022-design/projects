SDL- Transition Elements 2
1.Which of the following statements best describes the role of a catalyst?
It changes the chemical composition of the reactants.
It speeds up a chemical reaction without being consumed.
It lowers the energy released during a reaction.
It reacts with one of the reactants to form a product.
Correct Answer: B) It speeds up a chemical reaction without being consumed.
Explanation: A catalyst speeds up the reaction , but it is not used up in the reaction.
2. What is a primary use of gold (Au) in modern technology?
Manufacturing of electrical wires and components
Producing fertilizer
Making strong magnets
Producing thermometers
Correct Answer: A) Manufacturing of electrical wires and components
Explanation: Gold is an excellent conductor of electricity and is used in electrical connectors and high-end electronic devices.
3. Mercury (Hg) is commonly used in which of the following applications?
Making steel
Thermometers and barometers
Producing fertilizers
Electrical wiring
Correct Answer: B) Thermometers and barometers
Explanation: Mercury is liquid at room temperature and is used in thermometers and barometers because of its ability to expand and contract with temperature changes.
4. Which of the following metals is commonly used as a catalyst in the Haber process for synthesizing ammonia?
Gold (Au)
Silver (Ag)
Iron (Fe)
Mercury (Hg)
Correct Answer: C) Iron (Fe)
Explanation: Iron is used as a catalyst in the Haber process to help convert nitrogen and hydrogen into ammonia.
5. Why do transition metal compounds exhibit color?
A) They reflect all colors of light equally.
B) Their electrons absorb certain wavelengths of light and reflect the rest.
C) They emit light when heated.
D) They absorb all colors of light.
Correct Answer: B) Their electrons absorb certain wavelengths of light and reflect the rest.
Explanation: The color we see is due to the absorption of specific wavelengths of light by the electrons in transition metal compounds.
6. Which factor mainly determines the color of a transition metal compound?
A) The number of protons in the atom.
B) The arrangement of electrons in the atom.
C) The temperature of the compound.
D) The shape of the compound.
Correct Answer: B) The arrangement of electrons in the atom.
Explanation: The electron configuration and their transitions between energy levels determine the color of transition metal compounds.
7. Why does the color of a transition metal compound depend on the specific metal?
A) Each metal has different atomic sizes.
B) Each metal has unique electron arrangements.
C) Each metal absorbs all light equally.
D) Each metal has the same electron arrangement.
Correct Answer: B) Each metal has unique electron arrangements.
Explanation: Different transition metals have different electron configurations, leading to the absorption of different light wavelengths.
8. Which of the following transition metal compounds is commonly known for its blue color?
A) Copper sulfate (CuSO₄)
B) Iron oxide (Fe₂O₃)
C) Copper oxide (CuO)
D) Copper carbonate (CuCO3)
Correct Answer: A) Copper sulfate (CuSO₄)
Explanation: Copper sulfate is known for its blue color due to the specific electronic transitions in copper ions.
9. How is iron (Fe) used in steel making?
a) It is mixed with silver to create strong alloys
b) It is used as a catalyst in the chemical process
c) It is combined with carbon to make steel, an alloy which is strong and durable
d) It is used to produce electricity
Answer:
c) It is combined with carbon to make steel, which is strong and durable
Explanation: Iron (Fe) is combined with carbon to form steel, which is much stronger and more durable, making it ideal for use in construction and manufacturing.
10. Why is iron (Fe) used to make bridges?
a) It is very light and easy to carry
b) It is strong, durable, and can withstand heavy loads
c) It is resistant to corrosion
d) It is cheap and easy to replace
Answer:
b) It is strong, durable, and can withstand heavy loads
Explanation: Iron is used in making bridges because it is strong and durable, allowing bridges to support large amounts of weight and withstand harsh weather conditions.
