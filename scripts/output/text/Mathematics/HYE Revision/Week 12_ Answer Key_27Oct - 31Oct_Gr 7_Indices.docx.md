Monday – Multiplying the Same Bases
Rule: When multiplying powers with the same base, add the indices.
Tuesday – Dividing the Same Bases
Rule: When dividing powers with the same base, subtract the indices.
Wednesday – Power of a Power
Rule: When raising a power to another power, multiply the indices.
Thursday – Law of Expansion (Multiplying Different Bases with Same Power)
Rule: When two or more bases are multiplied and raised to the same power, distribute the power to each base.
