Answer Key — Ratios Worksheets
Day 1 — Understanding & Simplifying Ratios
Q1. Write the ratio and simplify
a) 12 boys : 16 girls
gcd(12,16)=4 → 3 : 4
b) 45 minutes : 1 hour
1 hour = 60 minutes → 45 : 60. gcd(45,60)=15 → 3 : 4
c) 75 paise : ₹3
₹3 = 300 paise → 75 : 300. gcd(75,300)=75 → 1 : 4
Q2. Basket has 24 apples and 36 bananas.
Apples : Bananas = 24 : 36. gcd(24,36)=12 → 2 : 3
Bananas : Total fruits = 36 : (24+36=60). gcd(36,60)=12 → 3 : 5
Q3. Simplify these ratios
a) 25 : 100 → gcd=25 → 1 : 4
b) 72 : 120 → gcd=24 → 3 : 5
c) 63 : 81 → gcd=9 → 7 : 9
Q4. Angles of triangle in ratio 2 : 3 : 4
Sum of parts = 2+3+4 = 9. Each part = 180° ÷ 9 = 20°.
Angles = 2×20°, 3×20°, 4×20° → 40°, 60°, 80°
Q5. Pens in ratio 5 : 7 : 8, total 100 pens
Total parts = 5+7+8 = 20. One part = 100 ÷ 20 = 5 pens.
Red = 5×5 = 25
Blue = 7×5 = 35
Green = 8×5 = 40
Day 2 — Ratio Word Problems
Q1. ₹4800 divided between A and B in ratio 5 : 7
Total parts = 5+7 = 12. One part = 4800 ÷ 12 = 400.
A’s share = 5×400 = ₹2000
B’s share = 7×400 = ₹2800
Q2. Rectangle with length : breadth = 3 : 2 and perimeter = 100 cm
Let L = 3x, B = 2x. Perimeter = 2(L + B) = 2(5x) = 10x = 100 → x = 10.
Length = 3×10 = 30 cm
Breadth = 2×10 = 20 cm
Q3. Boys : Girls = 7 : 5, total students = 360
Total parts = 7+5 = 12. One part = 360 ÷ 12 = 30.
Boys = 7×30 = 210
Girls = 5×30 = 150
Q4. Recipe flour : sugar = 4 : 3; flour used = 28 cups
4 parts = 28 → one part = 28 ÷ 4 = 7.
Sugar = 3×7 = 21 cups
Q5. Monthly incomes in ratio 9 : 11; difference = ₹4000
Difference in parts = 11 − 9 = 2 parts = ₹4000 → one part = 4000 ÷ 2 = 2000.
First friend = 9×2000 = ₹18,000
Second friend = 11×2000 = ₹22,000
