Day 1
Profit & Loss: A shop buys a watch for ₹1,200 and sells it for ₹1,500. Calculate the profit and the profit percentage.
GST: An electronic item costs ₹2,000. If GST is 18%, calculate the final price including GST.

Irrational Number: Identify the irrational number: √16, √5, 0.25, 1/3

Day 2
