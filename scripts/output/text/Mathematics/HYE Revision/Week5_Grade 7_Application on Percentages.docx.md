Q25. (Combined Word Problem)
A laptop has a price tag of ₹60,000. During a festival sale:
First, a discount of 15% is given.
Then, GST of 18% is applied on the reduced price.

a) The price after discount.
b) The GST amount.
c) The final price after discount and GST.
