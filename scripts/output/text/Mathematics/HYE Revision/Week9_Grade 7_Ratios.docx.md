Day 1 - Ratios Basics
Worksheet: Understanding and Simplifying Ratios
Write the ratio of:
a) 12 boys to 16 girls
b) 45 minutes to 1 hour

Write the ratio of apples to bananas in simplest form.

a) 25:100
b) 72:120
c) 63:81

Find each angle.

Day 2 – Ratio Applications
Worksheet: Word Problems on Ratios
A sum of ₹4800 is divided between A and B in the ratio 5:7.
Find A’s share and B’s share.
The ratio of length to breadth of a rectangle is 3:2. If the perimeter is 100 cm, find the length and breadth.
In a school, the ratio of boys to girls is 7:5. If there are 360 students in total, find the number of boys and girls.
A recipe calls for flour and sugar in the ratio 4:3. If a baker uses 28 cups of flour, how much sugar will he need?
The monthly incomes of two friends are in the ratio 9:11. If the difference in their incomes is ₹4000, find their individual incomes.
