a) A whole number
b) A natural number
c) Both (a) and (b)
d) None of these

Riya counted the number of chairs in the classroom and wrote the answer as 24.
Is her answer a natural number or a whole number? Explain why.

b) √49

If π is taken as 3.14, is the result rational or irrational?
