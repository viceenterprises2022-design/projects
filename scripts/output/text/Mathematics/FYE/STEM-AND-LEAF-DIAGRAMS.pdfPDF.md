1.

a school.
(a) How many teachers work at the school?
(b) What is the range of the ages of the teachers at the school?
(c) How many teachers are less than 35 years old?
(d) What is the mode?

(a) What is the mode?
(b) What is the range of the weights?
(c) What is the median weight?
(d) How many dogs weigh more than 35 kilograms?

last season.

11.
The stem and leaf diagram shows the scores achieved by 21 players in the first round of
a golf tournament.
(a) The par score for the course is 73. How many of the players scored below par?
(b) What was the median score?
(c) What was the range of scores?
(d) An extra player is added to the tournament. He scores 69 in his first round. Does this
change the median or the range that were calculated in parts (b) and (c)?
12.

(c) What was the median number of visitors?
(d) On the 15th day, there were 120 visitors to the launderette. Does this change the
median or the range that were calculated in parts (b) and (c)?
