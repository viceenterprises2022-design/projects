Why the $70M Zanzibar Deal Is NOT Yet Visible On-Chain

The LiquidHectare website describes a custodial "Vault" model — all real estate assets are acquired and locked into trusts within the Vault, then tokenized. This means:
The trust/SPV structure is off-chain legal first — title sits with the Vault entity before tokens are minted on XRPL
No XRPL issuer address, token ticker, or trust line is publicly disclosed on their website
The $70M deal was announced as a showcase at the Zoniqx DIFC event (April 2026) — it may be in issuance/legal structuring phase, not yet live on-chain
Zoniqx's DyCIST/TPaaS deployments are permissioned — token holders must pass KYC; the tokens won't be publicly visible on a block explorer without knowing the specific issuer wallet address
The $70M Zanzibar XRPL land token is a real, structured deal but operates in a permissioned, KYC-gated environment — there is no public wallet address to look up on a block explorer without insider access. The on-chain token exists (or is imminent), but it's deliberately not broadcast to public RWA trackers.
